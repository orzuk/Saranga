Your paper

Build the graph of any paper from its LaTeX source, with the same rule-based extractor that built the 100K corpus (the LaTeX parser plus the distilled sentence rules; no language model). It takes a few seconds.

From arXiv

The LaTeX source is downloaded from arXiv. Papers with only a PDF on arXiv are not supported.

Upload LaTeX

A single .tex file, or a .zip / .tar.gz of the LaTeX project (up to 50 MB). Uploads are kept for 24 hours, are visible only through their own link, and are never compiled.
