SARANGA

Turn any math or CS paper into an interactive graph of its definitions, theorems, proofs and how they depend on each other, in seconds.

The same agent extraction that built our silver corpus: a Claude agent reads the LaTeX, writes the graph and checks it until it validates. See examples of LLM extraction here (200 papers, each next to its rule-based graph). Your key and paper go to Anthropic under your own account, only for this run. We never store or log the key; an email address is used once to send the results and not kept.
