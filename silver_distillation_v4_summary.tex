\section*{Table 1 --- Matching metric: Jaccard vs Containment (paper001 only)}

\noindent
The default matcher uses set-Jaccard $|A \cap B| / |A \cup B|$ on lightly-normalized tokens. We added \texttt{clean\_latex\_to\_text} cleaning (drops braces, expands macros to readable text, strips \texttt{\textbackslash label/\textbackslash ref/\textbackslash cite}) and made the metric switchable. Containment $|A \cap B| / \min(|A|,|B|)$ is forgiving when the rules parser extracts a fragment of the opus narrative.

\medskip
\begin{tabular}{lC{2.8cm}C{2.8cm}C{2.8cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{Stage} & \textbf{Raw Jaccard} & \textbf{Clean Jaccard} & \textbf{Clean Containment} \\
\midrule
Baseline P / R / F1 & 0.00 / 0.00 / \textbf{0.000} & --- & 0.35 / 0.50 / \textbf{0.414} \\
Post-distill P / R / F1 & 0.13 / 0.33 / \textbf{0.182} & --- & 0.43 / 0.92 / \textbf{0.579}$^\dagger$ \\
Recall @ 0.7 (paper001) & 0 / 12 & 1 / 12 & 5 / 12 \\
Dark-knowledge items & 12 & --- & 7 (5 already matched) \\
Distilled rules generated & 22 & --- & 13 \\
Total cost (USD) & \$0.46 & --- & \$0.32 \\
Wall time (s) & 269 & --- & 193 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\medskip
\noindent
{\small $^\dagger$ Pre-prune, paper001 solo run. Set-Jaccard collapses every match for asymmetric spans (opus extracts 45--83-token paragraphs; rules extracts 17--37-token fragments). Containment captures 4 true matches that Jaccard misses (e.g.\ R5 ``Unnormalized Laplacian $L=D-W$'' $\leftrightarrow$ opus O7 ``$\backslash$Lun $= D-W$''). Cleaning alone only recovers 1 of those 4. \textbf{Adopted: clean + containment.}}

\section*{Table 2 --- Sequential distillation results (with FP-majority pruning)}

\noindent
Pipeline per paper: (1) parse with rules + silver distilled rules so far; (2) identify FNs (dark knowledge); (3) LLM-analyze each FN to extract a regex pattern; (4) LLM-aggregate cumulative analyze rows into \texttt{distilled\_rules.py}; (4b) deterministic prune of any rule where FP $>$ TP across all papers seen so far; (5) post-distillation evaluation.

\midrule

{\small \textbf{Key signal.} 1 of paper005's 3 baseline FNs was caught automatically by paper001's distilled rules --- direct evidence of rule transfer between papers. paper005's post-distillation F1 jumped from 0.250 to 0.750 (3 TP / 4 gold, 1 FP).}

\section*{Table 3 --- FP pruning impact}

\noindent
Without pruning, the aggregate LLM is incentivized to generate permissive patterns (it only sees FN signal). The deterministic prune step re-runs the distilled rules on all pilot papers, classifies each extraction as TP or FP, attributes FPs to specific compiled patterns by \texttt{rule\_name}, and comments out any rule where \textbf{FP $>$ TP} (gate added to avoid pruning net-helpful rules).

\medskip
\begin{tabular}{lcccc}
\toprule
\textbf{Variant} & \textbf{paper001 F1} & \textbf{paper005 F1} & \textbf{Avg F1} & \textbf{Active rules} \\
\midrule
No prune        & 0.550 & 0.500 & 0.525 & $\sim$20 \\
With FP$>$TP prune & 0.545 & \textbf{0.750} & \textbf{0.648} & 14 (3 pruned) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
